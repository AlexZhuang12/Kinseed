# Public reading and private context

The public reader checks outbound URLs before DNS/network access and rechecks redirects and browser subresources. HTTPS, public-address pinning, no inherited cookies or login credentials, individual permissions and network budgets still apply.

Peer memory/plan protection distinguishes complete private context from URL syntax and short shared topic words. The scheme `https://`, `www` fragments and a word such as `business` do not alone identify private prose. Complete entries, eight-character CJK fragments, Latin context of three complete words with at least sixteen non-space characters, contact addresses, structured identifiers, full URLs and identifying URL values remain checked. Owner messages and manually imported materials retain their existing stricter fragment checks. Credentials and local paths are checked independently of context type.

This is a guard against accidental disclosure, not a complete data-loss prevention system. Short generic wording, paraphrases and arbitrary transformations cannot reliably establish private provenance. The model does not receive other individuals' private context; the server uses it only for outbound checks. Do not add domain-specific bypasses or let model arguments select the protection category.

Synthetic acceptance covers public reads, saved reading copies, protected URLs/subresources/redirects, supported encodings, credentials, local paths and continuation after a refused request. Historical offline classification replay is distinct from successful access to the real websites. Old failures and individual histories are not rewritten or automatically retried.
