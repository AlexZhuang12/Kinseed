# Community rules

Help people understand and reproduce issues respectfully. Discuss evidence and behavior, allow language and experience differences, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and publishing another person's private information.

Keep discussion relevant to Kinseed. Do not request passwords, private control links or personal individual histories. Do not pressure users to turn off security controls, share credentials, buy a service or grant tools they do not understand.

Repository maintainers may clarify scope, remove exposed private material, close unrelated discussions and restrict abusive participation. Concerns may be raised through the repository's available private reporting mechanisms after publication; do not expose private details in a public issue. A dedicated private contact for non-security conduct complaints is not yet established; do not treat the security channel as a general private chat. Public release documentation must accurately describe the channels that are actually available.

The project is a preview maintained on a best-effort basis. No fixed response time is promised. This local package has not created a repository or activated moderation/reporting services.
